1. Overview

This Privacy Policy describes how Mike McKnight ("Operator", "I", "me") collects, uses, and protects information in connection with the McKnight Notification Service ("Service"). The Service is a private, personal test application that sends transactional SMS alerts to a single pre-configured mobile phone number owned and operated solely by the Operator.

This is not a consumer-facing product. No public sign-up exists. The Service sends messages to one recipient only: the Operator.

2. Information Collected

The Service collects and stores only the minimum information necessary to operate:

  • Phone number — A single private mobile phone number is hard-configured at deployment time. It belongs to the Operator and is never entered by or shared with any third party.
  • Operational event data — Internal application event records used solely to support the Service's logic. Stored locally on a private server and never transmitted externally.

The Service does not collect: names, email addresses, payment card numbers, location data, browsing history, or any data from anyone other than the Operator.

3. How Information Is Used

Collected information is used exclusively to:

  • Deliver transactional SMS notifications to the Operator's private mobile number.
  • Record internal operational events for the Service's own logic and state management.

4. SMS Messaging

Text messages are sent via Twilio to the Operator's private mobile number only. All messages are transactional system notifications generated by the Service on behalf of the Operator.

Message frequency varies depending on internal application activity. Message and data rates may apply.

To opt out of SMS messages at any time, reply STOP to any message. To request help, reply HELP. Opting out will disable the Service's SMS functionality.

5. Data Sharing and Disclosure

I do not sell, rent, trade, or otherwise share any information with any third party, except as strictly necessary to deliver SMS messages:

  • Twilio, Inc. — SMS delivery provider. Twilio processes the destination phone number and message body solely to deliver notifications. Twilio's privacy policy is available at twilio.com/en-us/legal/privacy.

No information is shared with advertisers, data brokers, analytics platforms, or any other party.

6. Data Retention

Operational event records are retained locally on a private server for as long as required by the Service's internal logic. No cloud backup of this data is performed. Data may be deleted at any time by the Operator.

7. Security

All sensitive configuration is stored as environment variables on a private server, excluded from version control. All outbound communication occurs over HTTPS/TLS.

8. Children's Privacy

The Service is operated solely for personal use by an adult. It is not directed at children under the age of 13, and no data is knowingly collected from children.

9. Changes to This Policy

This policy may be updated at any time. The "Last Updated" date at the top of this page reflects the most recent revision. Continued use of the Service after an update constitutes acceptance of the revised policy.
